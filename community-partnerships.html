Building a Community Through Meaningful Partnerships

SaraDharma is a residential wellness community for older adults in Bengaluru. We believe that the quality of life of our residents can be greatly enhanced through meaningful relationships with organizations in the wider community.

Our Partnership Philosophy

We welcome like-minded organizations to join us in creating a caring, healthy and meaningful environment for older adults.

Together, we can bring expertise, services, activities, compassion and community connection to improve well-being and enrich everyday life.

We are not simply looking for sponsors or service providers. We are looking for organizations that share our belief in creating a caring, connected and meaningful community.

Building Our Community Network

We are currently building our community partnership network.

Every partnership will be different. We would like to understand what your organization does best and explore how your expertise, services, activities or community initiatives could benefit our residents — and how SaraDharma could create value for your organization in return.

The best partnerships begin with a conversation and grow through shared purpose, trust and action.
