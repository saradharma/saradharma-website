Your Contribution Makes A Difference

Every sponsorship directly strengthens the SaraDharma Community and helps create a caring, healthy and spiritually enriching home for older adults.

Resident Sponsorship supports an individual resident by helping provide accommodation, healthy vegetarian meals, healthcare, companionship and daily wellbeing.

Facilities & Infrastructure Sponsorship creates lasting assets that benefit every resident for many years to come. Examples include comfortable rooms, exercise facilities, nursing care, renewable energy and essential community infrastructure.

Every contribution, irrespective of its size, helps preserve dignity, independence and peace of mind for our residents.
