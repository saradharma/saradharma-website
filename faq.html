Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about residency, facilities and community life at SaraDharma.

Who can apply for residency?

SaraDharma is primarily intended for senior citizens seeking a peaceful, value-based residential wellness community. Applications are reviewed individually.

What accommodation options are available?

We offer single occupancy, couple accommodation and shared residency options, subject to availability.

What facilities are available?

Residents enjoy comfortable accommodation, healthy vegetarian meals, yoga, meditation, terrace garden, walking areas, lift access, Wi‑Fi, CCTV, intercom and other amenities.

Is medical support available?

We work with nearby healthcare providers and emergency medical services to support the health and well-being of residents.

Can family members visit?

Yes. Family and friends are welcome to visit during designated visiting hours, ensuring a comfortable experience for all residents.

How do I apply?

Complete the Registration & Enquiry form. Our team will contact you, discuss your requirements and arrange a visit.

Can I sponsor a resident?

Yes. Individuals and organisations may sponsor residents, wellness programmes or infrastructure projects. Visit the Sponsor and Donate pages for more information.

How can I contact SaraDharma?

Please use the Contact page, email us or schedule a visit. We will be happy to answer your questions.

Still Have Questions?

Our team will be happy to assist you.
