Welcome to SaraDharma

"A Residential Wellness Community Inspired by Timeless Values"

At SaraDharma, we believe that every stage of life offers an opportunity to grow in wisdom, deepen relationships, and live with purpose. Inspired by the timeless principles of Dharma, SaraDharma is creating a warm and caring residential wellness community where senior adults experience dignity, companionship, holistic well-being, and the joy of helping one another.



Our Guiding Principle

Help One Another

“A caring community is built not only by the support we receive, but by the support we willingly offer to others.”

SaraDharma is founded on the belief that every individual has unique experiences, wisdom, talents, compassion, and kindness to share.

Through friendship, service, lifelong learning, volunteering, and community participation, residents continue to contribute to one another's well-being and enjoy a meaningful life.

Helping one another is not merely an activity— it is the culture that defines SaraDharma.
