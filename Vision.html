Understanding Today's Challenge

SaraDharma has been created to address one of the most significant challenges faced by Indian families today—providing a safe, caring and trustworthy home for ageing parents while preserving family bonds, dignity and peace of mind.

Resident & Non-Resident Indian Challenges

Whether living in India or overseas, many families struggle to find a comfortable residential community where aging parents receive dependable care and continue to enjoy an active, fulfilling life.

  • Safe and secure residential environment.
  • Professional care with personal attention.
  • Healthy vegetarian lifestyle.
  • Central Bengaluru location for easy family visits.
  • Comfortable accommodation for visiting relatives.
  • Peace of mind for children living abroad.
SaraDharma bridges the distance between generations by creating a trusted home where elders are cared for with dignity, compassion and respect.

Help-One-Another

Many of us have been supported throughout life by parents, teachers, mentors, relatives and lifelong friends. SaraDharma provides an opportunity to express gratitude by helping those who once helped us.

  • Sponsor parents or close relatives.
  • Support teachers and mentors.
  • Help deserving widows and widowers.
  • Assist senior citizens needing compassionate care.
  • Participate in community sponsorship.
  • Become part of the "Help-One-Another" movement.
Your sponsorship becomes a lasting expression of gratitude, compassion and service while enriching another person's life.

Vision • Mission • Today's Goal

Our Vision

To create a compassionate residential wellness community where older adults live with dignity, independence, purpose and spiritual fulfillment in a safe, peaceful and caring environment.

  • Luxury assisted living in Central Bengaluru.
  • A peaceful vegetarian lifestyle.
  • Respect for individual independence.
  • Spiritual and emotional well-being.
  • A caring "Help-One-Another" community.

Our Mission

SaraDharma is committed to providing an affordable, compassionate and professionally managed residential wellness community through community participation and shared responsibility.

  • Comfortable accommodation.
  • Nutritious vegetarian meals.
  • Yoga, wellness and healthy aging.
  • Meaningful friendships and community life.
  • Lifelong learning and purposeful living.
  • Gradually evolve into a non-profit model.
  • Support deserving elders through sponsorships.

Today's Goal

Build a trusted residential wellness community where every resident feels safe, valued, respected and connected.

  • Create a vibrant community of only 40 residents.
  • Provide individualized care.
  • Encourage active and healthy ageing.
  • Promote compassion, dignity and service.
  • Develop sustainable sponsorship programmes.
  • Create an environment families can completely trust.
