Small by Design. Rich in Relationships.

At SaraDharma, we believe quality of life is enhanced when residents live in a close-knit community where every individual is valued, respected and cared for. Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some of the most common questions about our facilities and community living.

Can family members visit residents?

Yes. Family members and friends are welcome during designated visiting hours. We encourage regular interaction and meaningful family connections.

Are meals provided?

Yes. Fresh, nutritious vegetarian meals are prepared daily with an emphasis on balanced nutrition and individual dietary needs where practical.

Are medical services available?

Routine wellness support, emergency coordination and tele-consultation facilities are available. We also work with trusted healthcare partners for additional medical care when required.

Can residents participate in daily activities?

Absolutely. Residents are encouraged to join yoga, meditation, gardening, reading groups, celebrations, volunteering and many other activities based on their interests.
