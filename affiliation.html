At SaraDharma, we believe that a vibrant community is created not only by the facilities within it, but also by the relationships it builds with the wider community.

Our Affiliation Philosophy

Working Together for a Better Community

We welcome like-minded organizations to partner with us in creating a caring, healthy and meaningful community for older adults. Together, we can bring expertise, services, activities and compassion to improve well-being and enrich life in every possible way.

Our Guiding Principle

“Help One Another”
This is the essence of SaraDharma.

What Does Affiliation Mean?

Affiliation is a mutually beneficial relationship that may include collaboration in services, activities, programs, events, volunteering, referrals, preferred access, community initiatives and more — based on shared values and goals.

✓
Joint activities and events for residents and the wider community.
✓
Preferred access or special arrangements where appropriate.
✓
Wellness, educational and cultural programs at SaraDharma.
✓
Volunteer and community-service initiatives that create social impact.
✓
Referral relationships based on trust and relevance.
✓
Philanthropic initiatives and services-in-kind where mutually appropriate.

Benefits of Partnering with SaraDharma

Benefits to Our Partners

  • Meaningful engagement with senior citizens
  • Preferred access to a trusted community
  • Brand recognition on our website and at our facility
  • Opportunities for collaboration and joint programs
  • Community goodwill and social impact
  • Potential customer, volunteer and community engagement
  • Potential tax benefits on eligible donations
* Tax benefits are subject to applicable laws and eligibility.

Benefits to SaraDharma & Residents

  • Access to trusted services and expertise
  • Potential cost savings and preferred arrangements
  • Better quality of life through programs and activities
  • Greater convenience and peace of mind
  • Opportunities for meaningful social and cultural engagement
  • A stronger, connected and caring community
